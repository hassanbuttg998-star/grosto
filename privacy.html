Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026
GROSTO par account banane ki zaroorat nahi hoti - isi wajah se hum sirf utni hi information lete hain jitni aapka order deliver karne ke liye zaroori hai.

Hum Kya Jaankari Lete Hain

Jab aap GROSTO par order place karte hain, hum ye details lete hain:

  • Aapka naam aur phone number
  • Delivery address (city aur poora pata)
  • Order ki tafseel (products, quantity, total amount)
  • Payment method (Cash on Delivery, EasyPaisa ya JazzCash) aur agar online payment ki ho to transaction ID

Hum Ye Jaankari Kaise Istemal Karte Hain

  • Aapka order confirm karne aur delivery karne ke liye
  • Delivery ke dauran aapse rabta karne ke liye (phone call/WhatsApp)
  • Payment confirm karne ke liye
  • Order history record rakhne ke liye, taake future mein order tracking mein madad mil sake

Jaankari Kis Ke Sath Share Nahi Hoti

GROSTO aapki personal information kisi third-party ko marketing ya kisi aur maqsad ke liye nahi bechta ya share nahi karta. Aapka data sirf order process karne tak mehdood rehta hai.

Data Security

Aapka order data ek secure database mein save hota hai. Sirf GROSTO ki admin team hi is data ko dekh sakti hai, order process karne ke liye.

Rabta Karein

Agar aap apni information ke baare mein koi sawal poochna chahte hain ya use delete karwana chahte hain, to hamein Contact page se rabta karein.
