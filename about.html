About GROSTO

Sub Kuch Milega.

GROSTO Gujrat, Punjab ke logon ke liye banaya gaya ek online grocery store hai. Hamara maqsad simple hai: aapko apne ghar baithe, bina kisi sign-up ya app install ke, apni roz marra ki zaroorat ki cheezein mangwane ka sabse aasan tareeqa dena.

Sirf kuch taps mein order place karein - naam, phone number aur address daalein, aur hum baaki sambhal lete hain. Cash on Delivery ya EasyPaisa/JazzCash se payment karein, aur aapka order seedha aapke ghar tak pohanch jayega.
